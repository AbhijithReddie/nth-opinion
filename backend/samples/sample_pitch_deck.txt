GharKaDabba (fictional startup for demos): home-cooked lunch, delivered to offices.

[Slide 2]
Problem: office workers in tech parks spend Rs 250+ on unhealthy lunches; home cooks have spare capacity but no customers.

[Slide 3]
Solution: an app that matches home cooks with nearby office workers for subscription lunch boxes.

[Slide 4]
Traction: live in 2 tech parks in Pune, 40 home cooks, 600 monthly subscribers, Rs 3.2 lakh monthly revenue (made-up numbers).

[Slide 5]
Business model: Rs 2,400 monthly subscription; we keep 20%. Delivery by our own runners.

[Slide 6]
Market: 1.2 million office workers in Pune tech parks; Rs 900 crore yearly lunch spend (illustrative estimate).

[Slide 7]
Competition: food delivery apps and canteens. Our edge: home food at canteen prices, fixed menu planning.

[Slide 8]
Team: two founders, one ex-operations at a fictional delivery company, one engineer.

[Slide 9]
The ask: Rs 40 lakh for 8% equity, to expand to 6 tech parks and hire 10 runners.
