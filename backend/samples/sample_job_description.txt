JUNIOR DATA ANALYST (FRESHER) - Example Retail Analytics (fictional company)
Location: Pune (hybrid)

About the role
You will turn sales and customer data into dashboards and insights for our merchandising team.

Must-have skills
- SQL: joins, group by, window functions
- Excel or Google Sheets: pivot tables, lookups
- One BI tool: Power BI or Tableau
- Clear communication of findings to non-technical teams

Nice to have
- Python (pandas) for data cleaning
- Basic statistics and A/B testing
- Retail or e-commerce data experience

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain weekly sales dashboards
- Answer ad-hoc questions from category managers
- Clean and validate data from store systems
- Present one insight per month to the leadership team
